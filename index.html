About the house

Old traditions,
modern hands

Georgia is a country that boasts an extraordinary history and culture — one that is inseparable from wine. GD Company preserves the ancient, unmistakable taste of Georgian wine while shaping its own incomparable style.

By uniting modern technology with the oldest traditions of winemaking, the house unites a premium line of wine with a classic line of brandy. Each release carries its own history, origin, character and appearance.

The distillery

A classic line of Georgian brandy

From the cellars of Kakheti, GD Company ages its spirits in oak to craft a classic line in the tradition of cognac. Warm amber, long resinous finishes, and bottles dressed for the occasion.

Two houses lead the range — Kakhetian Legends, matured three to seven years, and King David, a regal Georgian brandy offered as VS, VSOP and XO.

Georgian heritage

Eight thousand years under the vine

The story of Georgian wine is the story of a people — written across the valleys of Kakheti, in clay vessels buried beneath the earth.

The Alazani Valley

Sheltered by the Caucasus, the vineyards of Kakheti ripen under a long warm sun — the heartland of Georgian viticulture.

Born of Saperavi

The deep, teinturier Saperavi grape gives Georgia its great reds — from dry Saperavi to the celebrated semisweet Kindzmarauli.

The Kvevri

Fermented and aged in clay vessels — a UNESCO-recognised method, thousands of years old.

Oak & time

Brandies rest for years in oak casks, drawing out colour, warmth and a long harmonic trail.

For the world

Bottled in Georgia and carried abroad — heritage made for the tables of the world.
